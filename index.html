Trait MMAP

Trait MMAP (Mapper for Mammals and Parasites) is an interactive research tool for exploring mammal-parasite associations in a specimen-based framework. The mapper integrates information about mammal hosts, associated parasites, biological traits, collection localities, dates, and source records into a searchable visualization. Trait MMAP is designed not only for viewing occurrence records, but also for interacting with them as research data. Users can filter records by host and parasite taxonomy, host traits, geography, collection date, and data source; select records directly from the map; and download the resulting host–parasite associations for further analysis. Users may also upload their own host-parasite datasets for visualization within Trait MMAP. Uploaded data can be mapped, searched, spatially selected, and explored alongside the existing dataset, allowing the mapper to function as both a biodiversity data resource and a reusable visualization tool.
