Evidence-aware integration: independently cataloged MSB parasite specimens are labeled cataloged_parasite_specimen; KSB parasites retained as host parts are labeled host_part. The tracker searches them together without pretending they have identical provenance.
Current prototype limitations

MSB host metadata are still derived partly from information recorded with linked parasite records. KSB host data come from the earlier host-centered export. Authoritative host-record retrieval and a remote database/API backend remain planned.
